1. Who we are

88EST (88.hugotech.co.tz) is operated by HUGO TECH, Tanzania ("88EST", "we", "us"). We are the data controller for personal data processed through the platform. This Policy explains what personal data we collect, why, how we protect it, and your rights under the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 of Tanzania and its regulations.

Contact for privacy matters: info@hugotech.co.tz · WhatsApp +255 785 799 530.

3. Why we use your data

  • To provide the service: creating and managing accounts, publishing listings and seller pages, connecting customers with Sellers.
  • To verify and keep users safe: checking identity documents for the Verified badge, preventing fraud and fake listings, investigating reports, and protecting forms from bots.
  • To communicate with you: verification codes, password reset, account, listing, payment and security notices by email, SMS, WhatsApp or in-app messages.
  • To process payments for Pro and featured listings, and keep records.
  • To improve the platform: statistics on searches, views and assistant questions, mostly in aggregated form.
  • Promotional messages only if you opted in. You can opt out at any time.
  • To meet legal obligations and respond to lawful requests from authorities.

We process data on the basis of your consent, to perform our agreement with you (the Terms), to meet legal obligations, and for our legitimate interest in running a safe marketplace.

4. What is public

On your public seller page and listings, other users can see your name or business name, phone and WhatsApp numbers, general location (region, district, ward), biography, listings, ratings and badges. Listing maps show an approximate area only. Your email, password, office pin, NIDA number and verification documents are never shown publicly.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that help us run the platform, under confidentiality and security obligations: hosting and database (Railway), email delivery (our hosting provider), bot protection (Cloudflare Turnstile), and, for some assistant questions, an AI provider (Anthropic) that processes the message text to generate a reply.
  • Google Translate, only if you choose a language other than Kiswahili; the page text is then sent to Google for translation.
  • Authorities such as the police or courts, where required by law or to investigate fraud.
  • Other users, only to the extent described in section 4.

6. Transfers outside Tanzania

Some of our service providers store or process data on servers outside Tanzania. Where this happens, we take reasonable steps to make sure your data receives adequate protection as required by the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022, including choosing reputable providers with appropriate security measures.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account and listing data: while your account is active, and up to 2 years after closure for dispute and fraud handling.
  • Verification documents (NIDA, ID photo, selfie, licence): while your account is active, and deleted within 12 months after your account is closed, unless needed for an investigation or required by law.
  • Payment records: as long as required by tax and accounting laws.
  • Assistant chats, reports and technical logs: up to 24 months.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, access to verification documents limited to authorised staff, logging of administrator actions, and bot protection on sensitive forms. No system is completely secure; if a data breach is likely to put you at risk, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

9. Your rights

Subject to the law, you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your data is used;
  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • ask us to delete your data, or to stop or limit using it;
  • object to processing, including for direct marketing;
  • withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect processing already done);
  • complain to the Personal Data Protection Commission of Tanzania.

To use these rights, contact us at info@hugotech.co.tz. We may ask you to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

10. Children

88EST is not intended for people under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Cookies and device storage

We use your browser's local storage and a small number of cookies to keep you signed in, remember favourites, your region and language, and to protect forms from bots. We do not use advertising cookies.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Policy. We will post the new version on this page with a new date, and tell Sellers about important changes.
